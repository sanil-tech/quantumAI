## 1. Invariants
- **Invariant 1:** Automated processes cannot authorize live execution.
- **Invariant 2:** AI recommendations cannot authorize live execution.
- **Invariant 3:** Signal approval $\ne$ live execution authorization.
- **Invariant 4:** Dual control requires two distinct human actors (Reviewer A $\ne$ Reviewer B).
- **Invariant 5:** Time-bound expiration and emergency kill-switch revocation enforced.
